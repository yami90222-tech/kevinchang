考點 1：情緒形容詞 必考

-ed (人感到) vs. -ing (事物令人)

• interested in / interesting to
• excited about / exciting to
• bored with / boring to
• surprised at / surprising to

考點 2：三大因果句型 會考熱點

so... that... / enough / too... to

• S + be + so adj. that + 子句
• S + be + adj. + enough + to VR
• S + be + too + adj. + to VR (自帶否定)
⚠️ 注意：that 子句須保留受詞代名詞 it！

考點 3：數位素養與查核 新課綱素養
