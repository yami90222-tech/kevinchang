句型 A

Grammar Focus A：詢問過去是否有某個「經驗」

💡 句型 A 表達「從過去到目前為止的經驗」
❓ 疑問句 (Question)

Have you / they ever tried exercising?

Have you / they ever been to Taipei?

Has he / she ever tried exercising?

Has he / she ever been to Taipei?

✅ 肯定簡答與詳答 (Yes)

Yes, I / we / they have.

Yes, he / she has.

I / We / They have tried exercising (before).

He / She has been to Taipei (many times).

▲ 詳答部分可省略括號詞彙

❌ 否定簡答與詳答 (No)

No, I / we / they haven't.

No, he / she hasn't.

I / We / They have never tried exercising.

He / She has never been to Taipei.

常用縮寫表： I have = I've we have = we've you have = you've they have = they've he has = he's she has = she's have not = haven't has not = hasn't

💡 句型 B：詢問持續多久 (How long...?) 與 時間選擇 (for vs. since)

問句：How long + have/has + 主詞 + p.p. ?

⏳ for + 一段時間 例如：for ten years, for a month, for six months (計算總共多久)
📍 since + 時間起點 / 過去式子句 例如：since last month, since 2 p.m., since they were young (從何時開始)

句型 C

Grammar Focus C：事情做完了嗎？(already 已經 vs. yet 還沒)

💡 句型 C 表達「到目前為止已完成或未完成某事」
❓ 問句 (Question)

Have they had breakfast (yet)?

Has she lost five kilos (yet)?

✅ 肯定 (已完成)

Yes, they have. / she has.

They have (already) had breakfast.

She has lost five kilos.

❌ 否定 (未完成)

No, they haven't. / she hasn't.

They haven't had breakfast (yet).

She hasn't lost five kilos (yet).

✨ already：常放在 have/has 之後，主要用於「肯定句」。 ✨ yet：常放在句尾，用於「否定句」(表示還沒) 或「疑問句」(表示了嗎)。
