會考終極三大句型互換核心公式

課本 P.38 精選實例：水太冷了以致於我們無法在裡面游泳。

公式 1：too... to... (太...而不能...)

The water was too cold for us to swim in.

⚠️ 注意：動詞 swim in 後面不可加 it，因為受詞與主詞相同！

公式 2：not... enough to... (不夠...而不能...)

The water was not warm enough for us to swim in.

💡 將 cold 換成相反詞 warm，形容詞放 enough 前面！同樣省略 it。

公式 3：so... that... couldn't (如此...以致於無法...)

The water was so cold that we couldn't swim in it.

🌟 會考超級陷阱：that 後面為完整子句，介系詞 in 後必須保留 it！

Textbook P.35

Grammar Focus 1: 情緒形容詞速成

主詞是事物（令人...）用現在分詞 -ing；主詞是人（感到...）用過去分詞 -ed！

🎭

課本句型觀察 (Grammar Frame)

主詞為「事物」給人的感覺 V-ing (現在分詞)
The movie is interesting / exciting / boring / surprising (to me).

這部電影對我而言是「有趣／刺激／無聊／令人驚訝」的。(若要接對象用 to sb)

主詞為「人」因事而感到 V-ed (過去分詞) + 介系詞
I am interested in / excited about / bored with / surprised at the movie.

我對這部電影「感興趣 / 感到興奮 / 感到無聊 / 感到驚訝」。

⭐ 會考必考！過去分詞搭配固定介系詞：
interested in
excited about
bored with
surprised at

Speak and write 課本看圖實戰練習 (P.35 課本題目)

課本範例 (Example: interesting / interested)

The book is interesting to the children.

The children are interested in the book.

1 題目 1：exciting / excited
The man's idea is the people.
The people the man's idea.
2 題目 2：surprising / surprised
The reporter's question is the actor.
The actor the reporter's question.
3 題目 3：boring / bored (⚠️ 注意 last night 為過去式！)
整句完成 1：The TV show last night...
整句完成 2：The woman...
Textbook P.36

Grammar Focus 2: so... that... 因果句型

so + 形容詞 + that + 完整子句，表示「如此...以致於...」！

⚡
句型公式：
S + be/連綴動詞 + so + adj. + that + S + V (完整子句)

• I was so excited that I didn't think clearly. (我太興奮以致於沒有想清楚。)

• The kids were so scared that they couldn't fall asleep. (孩子們害怕到無法入睡。)

💡
課本【讀一讀】重點互換：
The movie was very exciting, so the students kept talking about it.
➔ The movie was so exciting that the students kept talking about it.

Speak and write 課本看圖實戰練習 (P.36)

課本範例 (Brad / thirsty / he drank a bottle of water)

Brad was so thirsty that he drank a bottle of water.

Textbook P.37

Usage A: enough... to... 的用法

「形容詞 + enough + to VR」表示足夠...以致於能夠做某事！

🍎
核心句型結構：

肯定：S + be + adj. + enough + to VR

Mrs. Hugo is old enough to be the actor's mother.

否定：S + be + not + adj. + enough + to VR

Zac is not old enough to watch the movie.

課本側邊常考補充

• on the tree：表示樹木本身生長出來的花、葉、果實（如 apples on the tree）。
• in the tree：表示來自外部停在枝葉間的物體，如鳥、風箏（birds in the tree）。

Speak and write 課本三題實戰 (P.37)

1. Lucy is very tall. Lucy can reach the apples on the tree.
➔ Lucy is reach the apples on the tree.
2. The new shoes are not big enough. Lucy's grandpa can't wear the shoes.
➔ The new shoes not for Lucy's grandpa to wear.
3. Lucy's grandma is very strong. Lucy's grandma can move the heavy boxes by herself.
➔ Lucy's grandma the heavy boxes by herself.
Textbook P.38

Usage B: too... to... 的用法

「too + 形容詞 + to VR」表面沒有 not，但本身即蘊含否定意思（太...以致於不能...）！

⚠️
課本句型觀察：

Kim is too young to understand the movie.

金年紀太小，以致於看不懂這部電影。（主詞是人）

The movie is too scary for Kim to watch.

這部電影對金而言太恐怖了以致於無法觀看。（主詞是物，加上 for sb，省略 it）

Speak and write 課本看圖實戰練習 (P.38)

課本範例 (the soup / hot / eat)

Alice can't eat the soup because it is too hot.

➔ The soup is too hot for Alice to eat.

1. Matt couldn't say anything... because he was too surprised.
➔ Matt was when he got the gifts from his classmates.
2. The kids didn't move because there was a snake in front of them.
➔ The kids were because of the snake.
