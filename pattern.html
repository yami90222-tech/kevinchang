文法大基石

什麼是 p.p. (過去分詞)？

在現在完成式中，我們一定要使用 Have / Has 加上動詞的第三個形態：p.p. (過去分詞)！
規則動詞只要加上 -ed，不規則動詞就需要特別記起來喔！

規則動詞 (加 -ed)
climb ➔ climbed ➔ climbed
try ➔ tried ➔ tried
不規則 (三態同形 A-A-A)
cut ➔ cut ➔ cut
read ➔ read ➔ read
不規則 (三態不同 A-B-C)
go ➔ went ➔ gone
drive ➔ drove ➔ driven

課本核心動詞 p.p. 配對小測驗

請選出正確的 p.p. (過去分詞)

💡 句型 A：詢問過去是否有某個「經驗」

問句：Have / Has + 主詞 + ever + p.p. ?

肯定：Yes, 主詞 + have/has. (S + have/has + p.p. once/twice/many times)

否定：No, 主詞 + haven't/hasn't. (S + have/has + never + p.p.)

💡 句型 B：詢問持續多久 (How long...?) 與 時間選擇 (for vs. since)

問句：How long + have/has + 主詞 + p.p. ?

⏳ for + 一段時間 例如：for ten years, for a month, for six months (計算總共多久)
📍 since + 時間起點 / 過去式子句 例如：since last month, since 2 p.m., since they were young (從何時開始)

快問快答：該用 for 還是 since？

請點選下面的時間詞，將它們分類到正確的口袋！

for (一段時間)
since (時間起點)

📸 情境看圖填空練習

know each other since they were in junior high school

Q: How long have Jane and Austin known each other?

A: They have known each other they were in junior high school.

be a mail carrier for six months

Q: How long has Jason been a mail carrier?

A: He has been a mail carrier six months.

💡 句型 C：事情做完了嗎？(already 已經 vs. yet 還沒)

問句：Have / Has + S + p.p. ... (yet)?

肯定句 (做了)：S + have/has + already + p.p.

否定句 (還沒)：S + haven't/hasn't + p.p. ... yet.
