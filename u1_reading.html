A Letter from a Teenager

💡 點擊任何一句話可單句朗讀；點擊黃色標記單字可查看中文解析！

Dear Debbie,

I'm a senior high school student, and I'm writing to ask for your help. My parents want me to stop playing music, but I don't want to. How can I make them understand that music is important to me?

I am good at playing the drums, and playing music gives me so much joy. My dream is to become a pop star one day, and I have just started a band with my friends. We've spent a lot of time practicing lately, so I haven't had much time for studying. I did terribly on my exams, and my parents were angry. Last week, we had a serious fight about my interest in playing music. My parents told me to give up my band and even called our music garbage. I have been so unhappy and haven't talked to them since then.

I know that my parents are worried about my future, but they have hurt my feelings. Music is everything to me. How can I talk them into letting me follow my dream? Thank you for reading my letter. I hope to hear from you soon.

Best wishes,
Ted

Envelope Exploration

英文信封書寫格式大解密 ✉️

英文信封的書寫習慣與中文不同！點擊信封上的各個區域，探索「寄件人（From）」、「收件人（To）」與「航空郵票（Stamp）」的標準書寫位置。

📬
寄件人 (From / Sender)
From: Ted Chin
No. 360, Nanmen Rd.,
Taichung City 402006
Taiwan (ROC)
郵票 (Stamp)
AIRMAIL
NT$ 9
郵政航空
收件人 (To / Recipient)
To: Ms. Debbie Bauer
    Teenagers' Corner
    No. 111, Spring Street,
    New York, NY 10012
    USA
1. 寄件人寫在「左上方」

包含姓名、門牌地址、城市、郵遞區號與國名（由小單位寫到大單位，如 No. 360, Nanmen Rd., Taichung City...）。

2. 郵票貼在「右上方」

國際航空郵件（Airmail）需貼妥足額航空郵票，讓郵局系統快速辨識國際分信。

3. 收件人寫在「右下方或正中央」

字體需清晰放大，尊稱（如 Ms., Mr.）明確，最後一行需為大寫國名（如 USA）。

應用字彙與關鍵片語寶典

點擊卡片可朗讀單字與例句，點擊「翻牌」可切換中英文！

Debbie 顧問的回信專區 (Dear Debbie's Advice)

如果你是 Debbie，你會給 Ted 什麼建議？點擊展開看專家建議：

Dear Ted,

Thank you for sharing your story. It's wonderful that you have found your passion for music! However, your parents are worried because balance is key in high school.

💡 Debbie 顧問的三大溝通心法：
1. Set a Study & Practice Schedule: 制定明確的時間表，證明你能兼顧學業與練鼓。
2. Listen to Their Concerns: 耐心聆聽父母的擔憂，理解他們的出發點是愛與關心。
3. Invite Them to Listen: 邀請父母聽你們用心練習的成果，讓他們看見音樂帶給你的熱情與成長！
