Q

邀約他人問句句型 (Inviting)

  • 模式 1

    Would you like to VR (原形動詞) with me?

    你想要跟我一起去...嗎？(最禮貌正式)

  • 模式 2

    Do you feel like V-ing (動名詞) with me?

    你想不想跟我一起...呢？(feel like + V-ing)

  • 模式 3

    Are you interested in V-ing (動名詞) with me?

    你有興趣跟我一起...嗎？(interested in + V-ing)

A

如何回應邀約 (Responding)

  • 欣然接受

    Sure. ______ is/are my favorite.

    當然！...是我的最愛。(例：Steak is my favorite.)

  • 客氣婉拒

    I'll pass. I don't like ______.

    不用了，我不喜歡...。(例：scary movies)

  • 另有安排

    I'll pass. I already have plans.

    不用了，我已經有其他安排了。

文法特訓：情緒形容詞 -ed vs. -ing 大對決！

人感到...用 -ed；事物令人...用 -ing！

會考必考重點
情緒動詞 -ed (人感到...) -ing (事物令人...) 課文經典金句
excite excited (感到興奮的) exciting (令人刺激的) The movie looks exciting. / I was so excited that...
bore bored (感到無聊的) boring (令人無聊的) I'll never be bored with them. / Scary movies are boring to me.
surprise surprised (感到驚訝的) surprising (令人驚訝的) I'm surprised to hear that. / That's not surprising.
interest interested (感興趣的) interesting (有趣的) Are you interested in watching it?
句型 1

adj. + enough + to V

足夠...以致於能夠...

"I'm old enough to be his mom."

句型 2

too + adj. + to V

太...以致於不能...

"I'm too young to watch it."

句型 3

so + adj. + that + 子句

如此...以致於...

"I was so excited that I didn't think clearly."
